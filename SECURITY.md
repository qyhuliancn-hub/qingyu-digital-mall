# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue containing credentials, order numbers,
license keys, customer data, or exploit details.

Report security concerns privately to **qyhulian@163.com** with:

- affected URL or component;
- a short description of the issue;
- reproduction steps;
- impact assessment;
- your contact information.

We will acknowledge the report and investigate it as soon as reasonably
possible. Please do not test against real customer accounts, real payments, or
data that does not belong to you.

## Scope

This repository is a product showcase. The live demo and licensing service are
operated separately:

- Demo: https://fkw.qyhulian.cn/
- License center: https://api.qyhulian.cn/

## Safe harbor

Good-faith security research that avoids privacy violations, service
disruption, data destruction, and financial harm is welcome. Do not use the
demo to upload unlawful content or attempt to bypass payment or licensing.
